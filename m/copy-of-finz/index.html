Your Tomorrow

GEorgiA Power

Your Tomorrow is a campaign for Georgia Power that brings to light the importance of going green today for the betterment of future generations. It addresses the need to appeal to wider audience bases and communicate the company’s sustainable initiatives. Your Tomorrow is a campaign proposal for Georgia Power, submitted as an entry in the SCADpro x GA Power 48-hour Design Challenge conducted in October 2021 and won the second place in the Challenge.

opportunity

How might Georgia Power showcase to the world, its commitment to sustainability and innovation?

solution

Your Tomorrow is an ad campaign that presents the opportunity for younger audiences to seize the day and design what their tomorrow looks like.

My roles

Research, UX Researcher, Business Strategist, and UX Designer

I maintained logistics and skill allocation to better define roles according to each team member's skillsets and capabilities. I designed the research plan, recruited and interviewed participants to gather data on financial practices and the emotions associated with the same. I ensured that the resultant service proposal conveyed the story of the target audience and identified the market spaces for the most impactful intervention. I built and prototyped the wireframes for the proposed app service, and usability tested the ergonomics of the application with different users to ensure a cohesive app experience. 

Research Insights

The research findings from both primary and secondary sources indicated that users face difficulty in finding money management solutions that work best for them. They also mentioned that they find it stressful to navigate financial services and their offerings due to the overwhelming amounts of available information.

Fin-Z

FinZ focuses on making monetary management easier for young adults by equipping them with the right tools best suited to their financial journey. The intent is to provide Gen Z a platform to learn, thereby empowering them to comfortably take the first step towards managing their money. 

​

Its key features provide the user simulations of real-life monetary situations and scenarios to better prepare every user for any plan or goal that they might choose going forward. For instance, the relocation simulator will help a user understand the different expenses associated with moving to a particular location while the investment simulator allows the user to learn the nuances of investing with fake currency in a risk-free environment, so that they feel empowered to apply these skills in a real-time investing scenario.

Business strategy 

The current market was analyzed to understand intervention spaces, specifically amongst the offerings of different competitors. FinZ is positioned among competitors such as Mint, NerdWallet, and Acorns, whose key features are mostly goal tracking and general financial management. The value that FinZ brings is its simplicity, both in usage as well as delivery of content. Its attention to detail ensures that the user receives quality information that is credible and curated to their specific needs.
